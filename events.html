Our events

Events

We host a variety of events throughout the year aimed at supporting foster youth, building community connections, and creating opportunities for mentorship, education, and giving. Each event helps us strengthen our impact and show foster youth that they are supported by a caring community.

Past and ongoing events

  • Laptop Project — all year
  • Annual Gift Drive — December, going on 3 years
  • Comfort Cases — twice a year, beginning of the semester and November 11th
  • Campus Tours — ongoing
  • FAPAC Summit — April 4
  • FAPAC DC Day — December 2025
  • Mentorship Opportunities — ongoing in the spring
  • Valentine Card Making — February 2025

Event highlights & impact

  • 2024 Giving Project: 70 gifts, $1,050 raised
  • 2025 Giving Project: 100 gifts, $1,500 raised
  • 2024 Comfort Cases: 30 backpacks packed for youth entering care
  • 2025 Comfort Cases: 60 backpacks packed
  • 2025 Flower Sale: $881 raised, funding 3 Dell laptops for youth pursuing their education
  • Supported Christmas for Kids in D.C., helping reconnect 300 siblings in care
  • Supported a Foster Youth Mentorship & Tour event with 60 mentors and youth
