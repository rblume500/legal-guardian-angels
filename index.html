What we do

We offer mentorship, legal support, and community resources designed to help foster youth feel seen, supported, and prepared for the future.

Our work blends volunteer service, education, and advocacy in a way that is welcoming and meaningful for both students and the youth we serve.

Why we exist

LGA believes strong education and stable support systems create better futures for foster youth. We work to increase access to resources, improve legal understanding, and build a caring campus community.

"Legal Guardian Angels has given me the opportunity to turn my passion for supporting foster youth into meaningful action within our community. As Director of Philanthropy, I’m proud to help create partnerships and experiences that remind youth in foster care that they are seen, valued, and supported."

— Rylie, Director of Philanthropy

Programs

Mentorship

Each mentor is matched with a youth through a thoughtful onboarding process. Meetings and check-ins build a consistent source of encouragement and practical support.

Court support

We help youth navigate legal appointments, explain what to expect, and offer companions when a trusted presence matters most.

Campus resources

Our network connects students and youth with housing, academic aid, financial support, and referrals to community partners.
