What we do

We offer mentorship, support, and community resources designed to help foster youth feel seen, supported, and prepared for the future.

Our work blends volunteer service, education, and advocacy in a way that is welcoming and meaningful for both students and the youth we serve.

Our Mission

LGA believes strong education and stable support systems create better futures for foster youth. We work to increase access to resources, improve legal understanding, and build a caring campus community.

The Numbers

The numbers are important. According to NCFA’s 2024 report, over 70,000 youth are waiting for a permanent family. Youth often spend more than two years in foster care, and out of 176,730 youth who exited care, 15,379 aged out of the system without finding permanency. This is why our mission matters.

Education & College Attendance

Foster youth face significant barriers to higher education compared to their peers. Under 4% of former foster youth go on to earn a four-year college degree, and under 6% receive a two-year degree. Organizations like ours work to close this gap by encouraging children in foster care to pursue education and connecting them with the resources they need to succeed.

Source: National Foster Youth Institute

Poverty’s Impact on Foster Care

Children living in poverty enter the foster care system at higher rates than children who are not in poverty. Poverty and neglect together have lasting negative effects on childhood development, and children in foster care are found to experience higher rates of mental and behavioral health challenges. Our mission is to combat this by supporting foster youth in every way we can.

Source: ScienceDirect
