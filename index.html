About us

What we do

We offer mentorship, support, and community resources designed to help foster youth feel seen, supported, and prepared for the future.

Our work blends volunteer service, education, and advocacy in a way that is welcoming and meaningful for both students and the youth we serve.

The numbers

The numbers are important — and this is why our mission matters.

According to NCFA's 2024 report, over 70,000 youth are waiting for a permanent family. Youth often spend more than two years in foster care, and out of 176,730 youth who exited care, 15,379 aged out of the system without finding permanency.

Education & College Attendance

Foster youth face significant barriers to higher education compared to their peers. Under 4% of former foster youth go on to earn a four-year college degree, and under 6% receive a two-year degree.

Source: National Foster Youth Institute

Poverty's Impact on Foster Care

Children living in poverty enter the foster care system at higher rates than children who are not in poverty. Poverty and neglect together have lasting negative effects on childhood development.

Source: ScienceDirect
