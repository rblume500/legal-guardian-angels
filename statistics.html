The numbers

The numbers show why our mission matters.

According to NCFA's 2024 report, over 70,000 youth are waiting for a permanent family, and many face major barriers to stability and opportunity.

The challenge is real — and so is the need for support.

Education & College Attendance

Foster youth face significant barriers to higher education compared to their peers. Under 4% of former youth in foster care go on to earn a four-year college degree, and under 6% receive a two-year degree.

Source: National Foster Youth Institute

Poverty's Impact on Foster Care

Children living in poverty enter the foster care system at higher rates than children who are not in poverty. Poverty and neglect together have lasting negative effects on childhood development.

Source: ScienceDirect
