Programs

Our programs are designed to create meaningful support for youth in foster care through volunteer work, education, community connection, and practical resources.

Fellowship Program

Fellows are a pool of committed members who work directly with our executive board to drive our mission and impact. At the end of the semester, a fellow will have the opportunity to collaborate on projects and initiatives that align with their interests and the organization's goals.

International Rescue Committee Mentors

IRC mentors provide guidance and support recently resettled youth with college and career exploration, goal setting, and post secondary preparation .

Off-campus Events

LGA participates in various off-campus events to raise awareness and engage with the broader community. We aim to build bridges between our organization and the wider campus and local communities by traveling to DC and working with larger organizations.

Comfort Cases

Comfort Cases provide essential items and support to youth entering foster care, helping them feel more comfortable and supported during a challenging time. This is one of our most anticpated events that we hold at the beginning of each semester. So far, we have donated over 90 backpacks to those in need.

The Summit

A free, one-day event in partnership with FAPAC for parents and youth ages 16-21 in DC foster care. By giving youth tours and pairing them with mentors, our goal was to inspire teens for life after the foster care system.
